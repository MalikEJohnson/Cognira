Institutional memory

Your company remembers why, not just what.

Search tells you where a decision was written down. Cognira reconstructs the reasoning behind it — the options that lost, who argued for what, and the assumptions nobody checked.

01 / INGEST

Read what already exists

Folders, Slack and Gmail exports, Word documents, PDFs, mail archives, GitHub commits and pull request threads. Nothing has to be written twice.

02 / EXTRACT

Pull the reasoning out

Each document yields structured decisions: what was chosen, why, which alternatives lost and on what grounds, who was involved, and what was assumed without being verified.

03 / ASK

Reconstruct, don't retrieve

Ask why something happened and get an account assembled across every source, cited and scored — or a straight answer that the record does not say.

Why it is built this way

A memory that guesses is worse than none

Cognira is instructed to say when the record does not contain an answer, to attribute every claim to the document it came from, and to surface disagreement between sources rather than quietly pick one. An organisation acting on a confidently fabricated memory is the failure this is designed against.

Pricing

One price. Your own workspace.

Paid in USDC on Solana, from the wallet you connect. No card, no account, no billing details stored anywhere.

These are passes, not subscriptions

Solana cannot charge your wallet again without you approving it, so nothing auto-renews and no payment method is kept on file. When a pass ends, access simply stops.

Renewing early loses you nothing

Buying while a pass is still live adds the new time onto the end of the old one rather than replacing it.

Connecting a wallet is not a payment

Signing in asks your wallet to sign a message. That proves you own the address — it moves no funds, costs no fee, and authorises no transaction.
